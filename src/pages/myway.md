# My Way

## Made for myself

This is the cookbook I wanted and could not find: the recipes I actually cook, the way I actually
cook them. No childhood anecdotes, no SEO filler, no ads pretending to be recipes. Just the food
and a few extras that make cooking less annoying.

I made it for myself but you are welcome here too. The recipe list is scarce, but growing. I doubt
I'll carry most of your favourite dishes, but hey, at least I will not ask you to subscribe to a
newsletter, or track your activity across the site.

## Made for a reason

### Everything I need is in the step

The quantities are listed in the step, and they scale with the number of services. No scrolling up
and down to remind myself of how much butter goes into the saucepan mid-cooking.

### Timers that stay with me

Tap a duration in any step to start a timer. Several timers can run at once and they keep counting
while I move between recipes, so I don't have to remember which timer I had for the rice and which
for the onions. And big buttons that I can hit with my wet fingers, my knuckles or, every so often,
my nose.

### The screen stays on

A running timer stops the display from sleeping. Waking a phone with greasy hands is the single most
annoying thing about cooking with one.

### Plain text recipes

Every recipe is a [CookLang](https://cooklang.org) file in a [Git
repository](https://github.com/goblindegook/now-we-try-it-my-way). No database maintenance headaches,
just simple text and a static website. The source code is free to access and modify, and anyone can
easily port my recipes to another app that supports the CookLang format.

### No accounts, no tracking, no ads

There is no analytics script, nothing to log in to, no advertising. This is the default for most of
my personal projects. Everything the site remembers --- the servings, the timers, the ticked-off
steps --- never leaves the device.
